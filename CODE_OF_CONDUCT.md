# Contributor Covenant — Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone contributing to ZYKO — through code, issues, docs, translations or community
support — deserves a harassment-free experience, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

**Positive behaviour**: empathy and patience, respectful disagreement, graceful
error-correcting, and language that welcomes newcomers (in Arabic or English —
both are first-class languages in this project).

**Unacceptable behaviour**: sexualised language, trolling, personal or political
attacks, publishing others' private information without consent, and any conduct
that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behaviour may be reported to the maintainers through
[GitHub contact](https://github.com/tibatechapp-ship-it/zyko) or the private
security channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). All complaints are reviewed and
handled confidentially.

Maintainers who do not follow this Code of Conduct in good faith face temporary
or permanent repercussions determined by the project leadership.
